How face verification works

  • Registering a face: a manager takes three photos of the worker with the tablet camera, after the worker agrees. The app keeps a numeric "face template" (128 numbers per photo) and one small reference photo.
  • Not registered yet? The worker's first clock-in photo becomes their reference. That shift waits as New face until a manager confirms it here.
  • At every clock-in and clock-out the camera photo is compared with the registered face. If it doesn't match, the clock-in is blocked and listed above under "Blocked attempts". A manager can still add the attendance by hand with a reason.
  • No camera or face check unavailable: the clock-in is allowed but held as "Face not checked" so a manager can review it.
  • Privacy: face templates are biometric data (UK GDPR special category). Record consent, keep a DPIA, delete the face data when a worker leaves (button on each row), and keep photos only as long as Settings allows.

      How hours are calculated

      The same rules as the payroll spreadsheet.

      • Clock-in: 1–3 minutes past the hour rounds down to the hour; 31–33 past rounds to the half hour; anything else rounds up to the next 15 minutes.
      • Clock-out: rounds to the nearest 15 minutes.
      • Paid hours = rounded out − rounded in, minus the break when the shift is longer than the threshold in Settings.
      • Rota: each clock-in is matched with that person's shift on the uploaded rota for the day (overnight shifts belong to the day they start).
      • Early: arriving before the rota start is paid from the rota start. Late leaving: staying after the rota end is paid to the rota end. A manager can approve the extra minutes with "Pay early" / "Pay overtime".
      • Late = rounded clock-in after the rota start, beyond the tolerance in Settings — sent for approval.
      • Face: a face that doesn't match the registered worker is blocked. New faces and clock-ins without a camera wait for a manager.
      • Needs approval: not a registered worker, not on the rota that day, new face to confirm, face not checked, late beyond the tolerance, left early, face-check flag, device clock moved back, or a shift that is too long or too short (limits in Settings). These are left out of payroll until approved, and listed on a “Held – not paid” sheet in the export.
      • Breaks: a clock-out followed by a clock-in within the break window counts as one shift (first in to last out) with the fixed break deduction.
      • Forgot to clock out: after the limit in Settings the person can clock in again; the old clock-in is listed for a manager to add the clock-out.
      • Locked days: exporting payroll offers to lock the day so its hours can’t change. Unlocking needs a reason and is logged.
